title: "Why don’t we penalize the intercept?"
author: "Conner Jensen (connerj2)"

Ridge usually penalizes the slope coefficients, but leaves the intercept unpenalized.

If we also shrank $\beta_0$ toward zero, what would go wrong for the fitted mean? Why is it enough to center $y$ and leave the intercept out of the penalty?
