Suppose we start with predictors $X_1,X_2$ and then add an exact copy $X_3=X_1$.

In Euclidean KNN, does that extra copy change who the nearest neighbors are? Why can a duplicated or highly correlated variable pull distance toward one coordinate even if it adds no new information?
